hand-tracker

Hand Tracker

It tracks your hand in real time and mimics your movements with two servo motors. A Raspberry Pi streams the video to your PC where MediaPipe calculates your hand position and the coordinates are sent right back to the Rasperry PI where it moves the two servos.

Hardware

The physical components that make up the tracking system.

Raspberry Pi 4

Captures video via the camera module and controls the servo motors through GPIO. The Pi handles all the hardware interactions.

The two servo motors

Two servo motors are mounted on a cardboard bracket so nothings tips over when the motors move.

Camera Module

The camera is connected to the Rasperry pi and captures the video

Why MediaPipe runs on the PC

Running MediaPipe hand detection on a separate PC instead of the Raspberry Pi is a important choice in my case.

Heat Management

MediaPipe hand detection is really intensive and generates much heat so running it on the Rasperry PI was not an option in my Case so I had to move MediaPiPe to my much more powerful PC to handle it.

Better Performance

My PC has a more powerful CPU, resulting in faster MediaPiPe perfomance. This keeps the frame rate high and steady
